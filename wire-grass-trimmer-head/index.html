Compare trimmer head configurations

Identify your machine

Provide the machine make and model, power source and a clear photo of its mounting. Diameter alone does not establish compatibility.

Specify the head

Compare twisted-wire, three- or six-bundle and nylon series. Record the outside diameter, bundle count where applicable, and mounting details separately.

Confirm suitability before ordering

Ask Frank to confirm the exact head specification. Follow the equipment manufacturer’s permitted attachments, guard requirements and speed limits before use.

Confirm trimmer compatibility before bulk purchasing

For wire grass trimmer heads, matching the machine and its mounting interface is essential. Compare head diameter, bundle configuration, attachment method and the manufacturer-approved operating conditions. Do not assume a head is compatible solely because the outer diameter matches.

What should distributors check before ordering?

Provide the machine brand and model, mounting dimensions or photos, preferred head type, target quantity and packing requirements. YEKAI can review the requested configuration before quotation.
