Send the workpiece material, its current surface condition and the finish you want. Include a photo or sample reference so the grit can be discussed for your application.

Match the catalog model

Compare wheel, cup and mounted shapes, then identify the outside diameter and mounting dimensions. The catalog table lists individual models, not every possible combination of diameter and grit.

Selecting abrasive filament brushes for wholesale orders

Abrasive nylon filament brushes are specified by brush shape, outside diameter, filament abrasive and grit, and mounting style. Choose a wheel, cup or shank-mounted design based on the contact area and machine. Ask for a sample or the current catalog before confirming an order, and verify the actual operating-speed limit for each model.

Which details help select an abrasive filament brush?

Tell us the workpiece material, intended finishing or deburring task, brush size, grit preference, machine type and planned order volume. Model availability and exact specifications must be confirmed before quotation.
