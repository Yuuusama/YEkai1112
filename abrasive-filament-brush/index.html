Selecting abrasive filament brushes for wholesale orders

Abrasive filament brushes are specified by brush shape, outside diameter, filament abrasive and grit, and mounting style. Choose a wheel, cup or shank-mounted design based on the contact area and machine. Ask for a sample or the current catalog before confirming an order, and verify the actual operating-speed limit for each model.

Which details help select an abrasive filament brush?

Tell us the workpiece material, intended finishing or deburring task, brush size, grit preference, machine type and planned order volume. Model availability and exact specifications must be confirmed before quotation.
