Buying guide: crimped vs. knotted wire brushes

Crimped wire brushes are commonly chosen when a more flexible brushing action is needed. Knotted wire designs are generally selected for more aggressive cleaning. Before ordering, compare brush diameter, wire material, wire thickness, mounting bore or thread, and the machine’s rated speed. Confirm the actual model specifications with YEKAI before use.
