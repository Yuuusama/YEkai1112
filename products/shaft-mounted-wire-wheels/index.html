Before you order

How do I distinguish a mounted wheel, cup and end brush?

State the head shape as well as its diameter. Include shank diameter and required access space. An end-brush diameter refers to its head, not the shank; list both dimensions in your request.

Frequently asked questions

What is a shank-mounted wire brush?

It is a wire brush with a shank that is held in a drill chuck. It comes as end, wheel and cup shapes, for holes, edges and small areas where an angle grinder does not fit.
