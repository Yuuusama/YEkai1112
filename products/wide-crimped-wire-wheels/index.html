Before you order

Is outside diameter enough to match a wheel brush?

No. Include working width, bore and machine details as well. State the exact metric or inch size you need; do not assume that similarly named sizes share the same bore or rated speed.

Selection and distributor purchasing

What to confirm before requesting a quote

When replacing a bench-mounted brush, provide the machine model, shaft diameter and available clearance. Do not infer the bore size or permissible speed from wheel diameter alone.
