Is outside diameter enough to match a wheel brush?

No. Include working width, bore and machine details as well. State the exact metric or inch size you need; do not assume that similarly named sizes share the same bore or rated speed.

Bench grinder version with reducing bushings

This is the thicker bench wheel: a wider face with more wire than an arbor-hole wheel, and stepped bushings in the hub so one wheel fits several shaft sizes. It is used mostly on bench grinders and costs more than the arbor-hole type. Shown here in copper-coated crimped wire.

Sizes: the same diameters as the arbor-hole wheel: 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 12 in (100 to 300 mm). Send the shaft diameter of your grinder and Frank confirms the bushing set.

Frequently asked questions

What is a crimped wire wheel for a bench grinder?

It is a wide-face wheel of crimped steel wire with an arbor hole that mounts on a bench grinder. It is used to clean rust, paint and burrs from loose parts. Series from 4 to 12 in, typical wire 0.3 mm.
