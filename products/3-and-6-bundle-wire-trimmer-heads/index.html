What should I include when choosing three or six bundles?

Specify bundle count and outside diameter separately, then provide machine and mounting details. Bundle count alone does not establish compatibility with a battery or petrol machine.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 3 or 6 bundle wire trimmer head?

It is a trimmer head with 3 or 6 steel wire bundles for clearing weeds from joints, edges and hard floors. The 5.5 mm bore fits battery trimmers and the 25.4 mm bore fits petrol brush cutters.
