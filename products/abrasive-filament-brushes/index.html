Before you order

How do I specify an abrasive filament brush?

Send the brush shape, outside diameter, workpiece material and desired finish. Add the abrasive type and grit if known. Frank will confirm the matching model and packing.

Selection and distributor purchasing

What to confirm before requesting a quote

For cleaning, deburring or finishing enquiries, specify the workpiece material, required finish and machine. Brush color alone does not identify abrasive type or grit.
