Frequently asked questions

What is an abrasive nylon filament brush?

It is a brush with nylon bristles that carry abrasive grain through the whole filament. It removes rust, corrosion, paint and primer while marking the part less than a wire brush. Available as wheel, cup and shank-mounted shapes, grit 80 (60/120 on request).
