Frequently asked questions

What is a stringer bead wire wheel?

It is a wheel with narrow, tightly twisted knots designed to follow a weld bead and enter bevels, removing slag and spatter. Series in 4, 4.5, 5, 6, 7 and 8 in, typical wire 0.5 mm carbon steel.
