More than 20 years making wire brushes

YEKAI is a wire brush manufacturer in Yongkang, Zhejiang, China's hardware manufacturing hub. The factory has been making wire brushes for more than 20 years and today has around 50 workers.

  • Main lines: wire cup brushes and wire wheels (knotted and crimped), shank-mounted brushes, wire weed brushes for brush cutters, abrasive nylon brushes and flap discs.
  • Private label: blister card, colour box or plain carton with your brand.
  • Terms: EXW, FOB or CIF; DDP to Mexico on request.

Wire materials and quality control

We make brushes in carbon steel, copper-coated steel, stainless steel (304 and 201) and brass wire. Brushes are checked on a balancing tester and every production batch is sampled before shipment.

Export markets

We export every year. India is one of our largest markets, alongside Russia and Japan, and we also supply many trading companies, so YEKAI brushes reach buyers in most regions of the world. We are now developing Mexico and Latin America and welcome new distributors in every country.

Trade shows

You can meet us in person. Every year we exhibit at the China International Hardware Show in Shanghai and at the Yiwu fair, we have exhibited at the Canton Fair in the last two years, and each year we plan a show abroad, such as Indonesia.
