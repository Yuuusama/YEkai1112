Wire weed brush for brush cutters and trimmers: types and how to choose

A weed brush (wire trimmer head) replaces the line or blade on a brush cutter or grass trimmer to remove weeds and moss from paving joints, kerbs, pavements and concrete floors without herbicide. There are three families in our catalog.

1. Twisted (knotted) wire weed brush

The most aggressive: knots of twisted carbon steel wire, 0.5 mm. The twisted wire series is listed in 6 in with 24 or 30 knots and 8 in with 24, 30 or 36 knots. More knots means a denser brush.

2. 3 or 6 bundle head

The 3 and 6 bundle heads come in 150, 180, 200 and 230 mm. There are two versions by machine: 5.5 mm bore for battery (cordless) trimmers and 25.4 mm bore for petrol brush cutters.

3. Nylon weed brush

The nylon trimmer brush uses rectangular nylon filament, in 6 and 8 in, with a 25.4 mm reference bore. Choose it when the surface is more delicate and should not be scratched by steel.

Fit is what causes most returns

There is no universal fit. Before ordering, send the make and model of the machine, the bore or shaft diameter and, if you can, a photo of the current head. Frank confirms the head, the adapter and the safe RPM for that model.

Safety

A steel brush throws stones and can shed wires: work with the guard fitted, a face shield, long trousers and closed footwear, and keep people away. On paving that scratches easily, test a hidden spot first or use nylon.

For distributors

This is a seasonal product for the growing season and for municipal and garden maintenance. It can be supplied under your brand in individual packaging; see how private label packaging works or the wire trimmer head category.

Frequently asked questions

What is a wire weed brush for a brush cutter used for?

For removing weeds and moss from paving joints, pavements and concrete floors, without herbicide.

Does it fit any brush cutter?

There is no universal fit. On 3 and 6 bundle heads, the 5.5 mm bore is for battery trimmers and the 25.4 mm bore for petrol brush cutters. Confirm make, model and bore before ordering.

Steel wire or nylon?

Twisted steel wire for tough weeds on hard surfaces; nylon when the surface must not be scratched.
