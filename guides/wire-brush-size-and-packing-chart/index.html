Wire brush sizes, weights and carton packing chart

This chart lists every size we have packing data for: weight per piece, pieces per export carton and carton size. We added two figures that importers usually work out by hand: the carton volume in m³ and roughly how many pieces fit in one cubic metre. Prices are not published here; ask Frank for a quote.

How to use the chart

  • m³ per carton = length × width × height in cm ÷ 1,000,000.
  • Pieces per m³ = pieces per carton ÷ m³ per carton, rounded. Use it to estimate how much of an LCL shipment or a container a mixed order fills.
  • Brush kg per carton = weight per piece × pieces. Add the carton and the inner packaging for gross weight.
  • Counts are for our standard packing. Blister cards, double blisters and colour boxes change the count per carton; Frank confirms the final packing list with your order.

Wire diameter: all crimped-wire models use 0.3 mm wire and all knotted (twisted) models use 0.5 mm. Abrasive brushes use 1.3 mm abrasive nylon filament, grit 80 as standard.

Frequently asked questions

How many wire cup brushes fit in one cubic metre?

It depends on the size. For example, 3-inch (75 mm) crimped cups pack 100 per 42×30×42 cm carton, about 1,890 pieces per m³. See the chart for each size.

Are these net or gross weights?

The weight is per brush and approximate. Add the carton and inner packaging for gross weight; Frank sends the packing list with your order.

Does custom packaging change the carton count?

Yes. Blister cards, double blisters and colour boxes change pieces per carton. The chart shows our standard packing.
