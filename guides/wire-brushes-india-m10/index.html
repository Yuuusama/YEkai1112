Wire brushes for 4-inch angle grinders in India: M10 thread guide

India is one of our largest export markets. Most of what we ship there is wire cup brushes, abrasive nylon brushes and flat wire wheels, mainly in 3-inch (75 mm) and 4-inch (100 mm) sizes. This guide covers the points Indian importers ask about most.

M10 thread: check the pitch

The 100 mm (4-inch) angle grinder usually has an M10 spindle, but M10 comes in two pitches: M10 × 1.5 (coarse) and M10 × 1.25 (fine). A 1.5 nut will not go onto a 1.25 spindle at all. Before ordering, check the grinders your customers use and state the pitch in the order. M10 × 1.5 is our standard; M10 × 1.25, M14 and 5/8"-11 are made to order.

Abrasive nylon brushes

Abrasive nylon filament brushes come as wheel, cup and shank-mounted models in grit 80 (60/120 on request); grit 80 is the most ordered. They are used for finishing, wood and parts that must not be scratched.

Flat wire wheels

Three types: the economical arbor-hole crimped wheel, the thicker bench wheel with reducing bushings and the knotted wheel. See how to choose a wire wheel for a bench grinder.

Wire options

Carbon steel, copper-coated steel, stainless steel (304 or 201) and brass wire. See which wire to use.

Frequently asked questions

What thread do 4-inch angle grinders use?

M10, in one of two pitches: M10 x 1.5 or M10 x 1.25. Check the spindle before ordering.

Which wire brush sizes are most common in India?

In our shipments, 3-inch (75 mm) and 4-inch (100 mm).
