Private label wire brushes: how OEM packaging works

If you run a hardware store, a distribution business or your own tool brand, you can order wire brushes with your logo and your packaging direct from the factory. This is how an order is put together at YEKAI.

Packaging types

  • Blister card (single or double blister): for peg displays
  • Colour box: for shelves and single-piece retail
  • Plain carton: for wholesale and industrial use

We also assemble wire brush sets on blister cards: you define the models and the pieces per card.

Printing plate cost

Custom printing carries a charge of RMB 300 per plate (per design). The charge is waived on orders of at least 5,000 pieces in total that combine 2 to 4 models on one printing plate.

Trial order

If you want to test the product first, we accept small orders in standard, non-customised packaging. Wholesale pricing does not apply to them and freight per piece is higher.

Step by step

  1. Choose models and sizes (what sizes to send).
  2. Ask for samples to check wire, mount and finish.
  3. Send your artwork: vector logo, texts, barcode and any importer details the label must carry in your country.
  4. You approve the design and the split by model before production.
  5. Production and shipping on EXW, FOB or CIF; DDP quoted by country.

What to send for a quote

  • Models, sizes and quantity per model
  • Packaging type and pieces per pack
  • Whether you have a design or need one adapted
  • Destination country and city

More on shipping in how to import wire brushes from China. Photos of finished packaging in OEM packaging.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to put my brand on the packaging?

RMB 300 per printing plate. It is waived on orders of at least 5,000 pieces in total with 2 to 4 models on one plate.

Can I order fewer than 5,000 pieces?

Yes. With custom printing the plate is charged; trial orders in standard packaging are also possible.

What packaging do you offer?

Blister card, double blister, colour box and plain carton.
