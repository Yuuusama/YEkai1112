How to stock wire brushes for a hardware store

You do not need every size to start. A small range covers most of what customers ask for; add sizes once you see what moves.

A starter range

ItemWhy
Crimped cup brush, 3" and 4"General cleaning on angle grinders; the everyday seller
Knotted cup brush, 3" and 4"Heavy rust and welds
Bench wheel, 6" and 8"For bench grinders in workshops
Shank-mounted set for drillsLow price point, sold as a set
Hand wire brushImpulse item at the counter

Products: crimped cups, knotted cups, bench wheels, drill brushes, hand brushes.

Match the thread to your market

Cup brushes must fit the grinders sold locally. Check whether your market uses M10 (and which pitch, 1.5 or 1.25), M14 or 5/8"-11 before ordering. See sizes and threads.

Packaging that sells

Blister cards hang on peg displays and protect the wire; colour boxes suit shelves. Both can carry your brand: the plate fee is RMB 300 per design and is waived at 5,000 pieces in total with 2 to 4 models on one plate. Details in private label packaging.

Start with a trial order

We accept small trial orders in standard packaging, so you can test the range before committing to volume.

Frequently asked questions

Which wire brushes sell most in a hardware store?

Crimped and knotted cup brushes in 3 and 4 inch, bench wheels in 6 and 8 inch, drill sets and hand brushes.

Can I order with my own brand?

Yes: blister card or colour box. The RMB 300 plate fee is waived at 5,000 pieces in total with 2 to 4 models on one plate.

Is there a minimum order?

Custom-printed blister cards start at 3,000 pieces per model; below that we supply our standard card. Small trial orders in standard packaging are accepted, without wholesale pricing.
