How to choose a wire wheel for a bench grinder

A bench grinder wire wheel works on its edge and cleans loose parts held against it: tools, fasteners, castings. There are three families, and they are not priced or built the same.

The three types

TypeBuildUse
Arbor-hole crimped wheelThinner, plain metal hubEconomical general cleaning
Bench wheel with reducing bushingsThicker face, more wire, stepped bushings in the hubMostly bench grinders; costs more than the arbor-hole type
Knotted wheelTwisted knots, stiffHeavy rust, scale, welds

The arbor-hole crimped wheel and the bench version with bushings come in the same diameters, 4 to 12 in, with 0.3 mm typical wire. For knotted wheels see the stringer bead knot wheel (4.5 to 7 in).

Diameter

Use the wheel diameter the grinder is built for: a 6-inch grinder takes a 6-inch (150 mm) wheel, an 8-inch grinder an 8-inch (200 mm) wheel. Never fit a larger wheel than the guard allows.

Arbor hole and bushings

Give the shaft diameter of the grinder. Bench wheels with reducing bushings fit several shaft sizes with one wheel, which is why resellers like them: fewer references to stock. Confirm the bushing sizes with Frank for your market.

Wire

Crimped carbon steel or copper-coated steel for general work; stainless steel wire for stainless parts; brass wire for softer metals. Typical wire in our bench series is 0.3 mm.

Tips for use

  • Use light pressure and let the wire tips do the work; pressing harder bends the wire and shortens brush life.
  • Run a new brush for about a minute inside the guard before the first job.
  • Hold the part below the centre line of the wheel and set the tool rest close to it. Turn the wheel around from time to time so the wire wears evenly.
  • Replace the brush when the wire is worn short or it starts to vibrate.

Safety

The maximum RPM marked on the brush must be equal to or higher than the machine speed. Use the guard, eye protection and gloves, and ask Frank to confirm the RPM of the model you choose.

Frequently asked questions

What size wire wheel fits a 6-inch bench grinder?

A 6-inch (150 mm) wheel. Use the diameter the grinder and its guard are built for.

What are reducing bushings on a wire wheel?

Stepped rings in the hub that let one wheel fit several shaft diameters.

Why is a bench wheel more expensive than an arbor-hole wheel?

It has a thicker face, more wire and the bushing set, and it is built for bench grinder use.
