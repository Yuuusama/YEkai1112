Wire brushes for stainless steel and aluminium: which wire to use

The wire material matters as much as the shape. The wrong wire can leave rust marks on a part that should never rust.

Why not carbon steel on stainless

A carbon steel brush leaves tiny steel particles on the surface. On stainless steel or aluminium these particles rust and stain the part later, sometimes days after the job. The same happens with a stainless brush that was used on ordinary steel before: keep separate brushes.

WireUse it for

Copper-coated wire and brass wire look alike but are not the same product. State which one you need.

When to use abrasive nylon instead

For a fine, even finish on stainless or aluminium with no metal contact at all, use an abrasive nylon brush.

How to order

Wire material is confirmed per model. Choose the shape first (cup, wheel, shank-mounted), then tell Frank the workpiece material and he will confirm the wire.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a carbon steel wire brush on stainless steel?

No. It leaves steel particles that rust and stain the part. Use stainless steel wire.

Is copper-coated wire the same as brass wire?

No. Copper-coated wire is steel with a coating; brass wire is solid brass and softer.

Which wire for aluminium?

Stainless steel wire, or an abrasive nylon brush for a finer finish.
