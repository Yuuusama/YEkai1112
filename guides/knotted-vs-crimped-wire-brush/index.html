Knotted vs crimped wire brush: how to choose the right one

A power wire brush is defined by two things: the wire style (crimped or knotted) and the shape (wheel, cup, bevel or shank-mounted). Settle both before asking for a price.

Crimped wire brushes

The wires are loose and wavy, so the brush is more flexible. Use it for light to medium cleaning: surface rust, loose paint and dirt, with a more even finish on wide areas.

In our range: crimped wire cup brushes (2.5 to 6 in), wide-face crimped wire wheels (4 to 12 in, typical wire 0.3 mm) and crimped wire brush wheels with a metal hub.

Knotted (twist knot) wire brushes

The wire is twisted into knots, which makes the brush stiffer and more aggressive. Choose it for heavy work: thick rust, mill scale, slag and weld seams.

In our range: knotted wire cup brush (2.5 to 6 in), banded knot cup brush (2.5 to 5 in), knotted bevel brush (4 to 5 in, typical wire 0.5 mm) and stringer bead wheel for welds (4.5 to 7 in).

Quick comparison

CrimpedKnotted
ActionFlexible, gentlerStiff, more aggressive
Typical jobLight rust, loose paint, general cleaningHeavy rust, scale, weld seams

Wheel, cup, bevel or shank-mounted

Wheel brush: works on its edge; wheels with an arbor hole usually go on a bench grinder. Cup brush: works on its face and threads onto an angle grinder, for large flat surfaces. Bevel brush: reaches corners and edges on an angle grinder. Shank-mounted brush: for drills, in holes and small areas (see shank-mounted brushes).

Safety

The maximum safe speed (RPM) marked on the brush must be equal to or higher than the speed of the machine. Always use the guard, eye protection and gloves. Ask Frank to confirm the RPM of the model you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Which is more aggressive, a knotted or a crimped wire brush?

Knotted. The twisted knots make it stiffer; a crimped brush is more flexible and leaves a more even finish.

Which wire brush goes on an angle grinder?

Cup and bevel brushes, which are threaded. Wheel brushes with an arbor hole usually go on a bench grinder.

Is a twist knot brush the same as a knotted brush?

Yes. Knotted, twist knot and twisted wire brush are names for the same construction.
