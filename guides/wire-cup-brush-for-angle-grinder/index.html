How to choose a wire cup brush for an angle grinder

A wire cup brush works on its face, so it is the fastest way to clean large flat surfaces with an angle grinder. Three things decide the right one: cup diameter, thread and wire style.

1. Match the cup to the grinder

Grinder sizeTypical cup brushTypical thread
100 mm (4")65 to 75 mm (2.5" to 3")M10
115 to 125 mm (4.5" to 5")75 to 100 mm (3" to 4")M14
180 to 230 mm (7" to 9")125 to 150 mm (5" to 6")M14

These are typical pairings, not a rule: the brush must clear the guard and its maximum RPM must cover the grinder speed.

2. Get the thread right

Check the thread pitch, not only the diameter. M14 spindles use a 2.0 mm pitch. M10 exists in two pitches: M10 × 1.5 (coarse) and M10 × 1.25 (fine), and many 4-inch grinders use the fine one. The two do not fit each other at all. 5/8"-11 is made to order.

3. Crimped or knotted

Crimped: flexible, for surface rust, loose paint and general cleaning: crimped wire cup brush, 2.5 to 6 in. Knotted: stiff and aggressive, for thick rust, scale and welds: knotted wire cup brush, or the banded knot cup when you want the knots held tighter. More in knotted vs crimped.

4. Wire material

Carbon steel is the standard for steel and iron. For stainless steel or aluminium use stainless wire; see wire brushes for stainless steel and aluminium.

Tips for use

  • Use light pressure and let the wire tips do the work; pressing harder bends the wire and shortens brush life.
  • Run a new brush for about a minute inside the guard before the first job.
  • Keep the cup face flat or slightly tilted on the surface; do not dig in with the edge.
  • Replace the brush when the wire is worn short or it starts to vibrate.

Safety

The maximum RPM marked on the brush must be equal to or higher than the machine speed. Use the guard, eye protection and gloves, and ask Frank to confirm the RPM of the model you choose.

Frequently asked questions

What size wire cup brush fits a 4-inch angle grinder?

Typically a 65 to 75 mm (2.5 to 3 in) cup with an M10 thread. Check the guard clearance and the thread pitch before ordering.

Are M10 x 1.5 and M10 x 1.25 interchangeable?

No. They are different pitches and do not fit each other. State the pitch in the order.

Crimped or knotted cup brush?

Crimped for light cleaning and loose paint; knotted for heavy rust, scale and welds.
