How to import wire brushes direct from a factory in China

If you run a hardware store, a distribution business or your own tool brand, buying wire brushes direct from the factory gives you better margin and control over packaging. This is how we work at YEKAI, a wire brush factory in Yongkang, Zhejiang.

1. Define your range

Start with the sizes you sell most, for example 3" and 4" cup brushes for angle grinders and wheel brushes for bench grinders. Split the quantity by size and wire style. The sizes guide lists what to send.

2. Ask for samples

Before a bulk order we send samples so you can check the wire, the mount and the finish. Samples travel by air; bulk orders by sea.

3. Choose the packaging

You can order a plain carton, a blister card or a colour box, with your brand. The printing plate costs RMB 300 per design; it is waived on orders of at least 5,000 pieces in total that combine 2 to 4 models on one plate. More in private label packaging and blister card sets.

4. Trial order

We accept small trial orders in standard packaging. Wholesale pricing does not apply to them and freight per piece is higher, but they let you test the product with your customers.

5. Shipping and duties

We quote EXW, FOB or CIF. If you prefer not to handle the import yourself, we can also quote DDP (door to door, duties included), for example to Mexico; it is confirmed by country, product, volume and destination city.

6. Payment, lead time and quality control

Standard payment is 30% deposit and the balance before shipment. Production of a bulk order takes 10 to 30 days, confirmed for each order. We check brushes on a balancing tester and sample every batch before dispatch.

7. What to send for a quote

  • Products and reference photos
  • Sizes and mount for each model
  • Quantity per model
  • Packaging type and whether it carries your brand
  • Delivery city and term (EXW, FOB, CIF or DDP)

With that, Frank prepares the factory quote. You can first see the factory and the documents.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are sent by air so you can check wire, mount and finish before the wholesale order.

What does DDP mean?

Door-to-door delivery with import duties included. It is quoted by country, product, volume and destination city.

Do you accept small orders?

Yes, trial orders in standard packaging. Wholesale pricing does not apply and freight per piece is higher.
