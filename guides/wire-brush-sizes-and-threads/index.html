Wire brush sizes: what to send for a quote

A fast, error-free quote depends on four things: outside diameter, mount (thread, arbor hole or shank), wire and quantity per size.

1. Outside diameter

Some markets order in inches and others in millimetres. Approximate equivalents:

InchesMillimetres (approx.)
3"75 mm
4"100 mm
4.5"115 mm
5"125 mm
6"150 mm
7"175 mm
8"200 mm
10"250 mm
12"300 mm

Each product page lists its available diameters. If the one you need is not shown, ask.

2. Mount

Cup and bevel brushes for angle grinders are threaded. Our standard thread is M10 × 1.5; M14 (2.0 mm pitch) and other threads are made to order. Note that M10 exists in two pitches, 1.5 (coarse) and 1.25 (fine): many 4-inch grinders use the fine one, and the two do not fit each other. 5/8"-11 is made to order. Check which thread your customers' grinders use and state it in the order.

Wheel brushes for bench grinders have an arbor hole. Give the shaft diameter of the grinder; if the hole is larger, bushings or adapters are used and are confirmed with the quote.

Shank-mounted brushes for drills: give the shank diameter and the head diameter. See shank-mounted wire brushes.

3. Wire

State crimped or knotted (see the difference), the material (carbon steel, copper-coated) and the wire gauge if you have it. For reference, our crimped wheels typically use 0.3 mm wire and the knotted bevel brush 0.5 mm.

4. Speed (RPM)

The maximum RPM of the brush must be equal to or higher than the machine speed. Frank confirms the RPM of each model before the order.

Checklist to send on WhatsApp

  • Photo or reference model of the brush
  • Outside diameter
  • Thread, arbor hole or shank
  • Wire: crimped or knotted, material
  • Quantity per size
  • Packaging: bulk, blister card or colour box, with or without your brand
  • Destination country and city

Frequently asked questions

What thread does a wire cup brush for an angle grinder have?

Our standard is M10 × 1.5. M10 × 1.25 and M14 are made to order (M10 × 1.5 and M10 × 1.25 are not interchangeable). 5/8"-11 is made to order. Confirm before ordering.

How many millimetres is a 4.5 inch wire brush?

About 115 mm. Others: 4" = 100 mm, 5" = 125 mm, 6" = 150 mm, 7" = 175 mm.

What do I need to send for a wholesale wire brush quote?

Outside diameter, mount (thread, arbor hole or shank), wire style, quantity per size, packaging and destination.
