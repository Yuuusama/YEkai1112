Specify flap discs for wholesale supply

Define the workpiece

Tell Frank which material you work on and whether you need stock removal or a finishing step. Confirm the abrasive type rather than identifying it by color alone.

Provide the disc dimensions

Include outside diameter, bore, grit and backing requirements. Send a label or sample photo when matching an existing item in your range.

Agree on packing

List quantities by grit and diameter and specify standard or private-label packaging. Confirm the product specification and label information before production.

Flap disc selection checklist for distributors

Compare abrasive type, grit, disc diameter, center bore and backing format before selecting flap discs for a market. The right choice depends on the workpiece, desired finish and machine compatibility. Request model-specific information instead of relying on a generic product description.

What information is needed for a flap disc quote?

Send the required diameter, grit range, center bore, backing type, quantity and target packaging. Confirm all technical specifications and operating limits for the selected model.
